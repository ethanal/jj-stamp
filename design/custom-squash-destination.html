01Save a rule, not an ID

Enter a revset and press Enter. It must resolve to exactly one eligible commit. A dashed arrow previews edits; applying makes it solid. A change ID works too, for a fixed target.

02@ is the working copy

@-- means two parents behind @, not behind the selected review. Move @ in the demo: the expression stays @-- while its resolved ID and the arrow change. Your review stays pinned.

03No silent fallback

Zero matches, multiple matches, or an ineligible target block squash. Queued batches keep their pinned destination; a moving expression never reroutes work already accepted.
