01A source-relative default

Parent always means the reviewed revision’s immediate parent—not the working copy’s parent, and not the next filtered log row. Changing reviews updates this default.

02One obvious dropdown

The bordered control combines mode and ID. Its lean menu offers Parent and Choose revision in log…. Parent clears the custom destination. No separate Change button.

03Open just long enough to pick

Choosing opens a hidden log temporarily. Hover previews; click or Enter confirms. Escape or Cancel restores the prior destination and layout. “Choosing…” and a dashed border distinguish the preview.
