01A source-relative default

Commit below always means the reviewed commit’s immediate parent—not the working copy’s parent, and not the next filtered log row. Changing reviews updates this default.

02Custom stays explicit

Select Custom, then click a commit in the log. The margin arrow previews your choice without changing the review. Enter confirms; Escape restores the prior mode and destination.

03A revset is optional

Custom also accepts a revset resolving to exactly one eligible commit. @-- follows the working copy only when you explicitly choose it. Queued batches keep their pinned destination.
